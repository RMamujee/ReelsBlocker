Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above and open the file when it's done.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (Android asks this for any app that isn't from the Play Store).
  3. Open SilentFeed and log in to Instagram. Your login goes straight to Instagram; this app never sees or stores it.

Updates: the app tells you when there's a new version and downloads it from this page. Install it over the old one and you stay logged in.

The app also includes an optional blocker for the real Instagram app, which needs Android's accessibility permission. Android blocks that for apps installed from outside the Play Store until you allow it: in Settings › Apps › Reels Blocker, open the ⋮ menu and choose Allow restricted settings. You don't need it to use SilentFeed.

iPhone Safari

Apple only allows apps from the App Store, so on iPhone SilentFeed runs inside Safari, using the free Userscripts extension.

  1. Install Userscripts from the App Store and open it once.
  2. In Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Userscripts, turn it on and allow it on instagram.com.
  3. In Safari, open the SilentFeed script, tap the Userscripts button in the address bar (the puzzle piece), and tap Install.
  4. Go to instagram.com in Safari and log in. Tip: Share › Add to Home Screen gives it an icon.

Privacy

There's no SilentFeed server and no account. The app is Instagram's own website with a filter on top: everything you see and send goes between your phone and Instagram. Nothing is collected or tracked. The only thing it ever asks this site is whether there's a newer version.

SilentFeed isn't made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta. Use it with your own account, under Instagram's terms.
